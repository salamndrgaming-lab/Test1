# NewsScope — Brand & Messaging

## Positioning statement
For **news consumers who distrust one-sided media**, NewsScope is a **bias-aware
news briefing** that shows **how Left, Center, and Right cover the same story** —
unlike single-feed aggregators, it makes media bias visible and gives you the
whole picture in one fast, mobile-first app.

## Taglines
- **See every side of the news.** (primary)
- News, without the echo chamber.
- Know what the other side is reading.
- Your whole briefing — every angle, plus markets, weather, and the game.

## Value props by audience
| Segment | Pain | Message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| News-fatigued moderates | "I don't trust any single source." | "Compare all sides at a glance and decide for yourself." |
| Engaged news junkies (L & R) | "I only see my side." | "See exactly what the other side is saying — and what yours is ignoring." |
| Educators / media literacy | "Hard to teach bias concretely." | "A live, visual tool for spotting bias and blindspots." |
| Busy professionals | "No time to read 5 outlets." | "One briefing: every side, plus local, markets, weather, sports." |

## Messaging matrix (hook → proof → CTA)
- **Blindspot:** "One story, three framings." → the 3-column comparison + the
  'blindspot' flag. → *Compare a story free.*
- **My News Bias:** "Is your media diet balanced?" → personal lean breakdown
  over time. → *See your bias (Pro).*
- **All-in-one briefing:** "Stop tab-hopping." → news + markets + weather +
  sports. → *Open the app.*

## Voice
Smart, calm, **non-partisan**, a little witty — never preachy or alarmist. We are
a *neutral instrument*, not a pundit.

**Do:** be concrete and visual; show, don't tell; cite; respect the reader's
intelligence; stay above the fray.
**Don't:** take political sides; rage-bait; overclaim ("unbiased truth"); bash
outlets or competitors; use doom language.

## Naming note
"NewsScope" is a working name — confirm trademark/domain availability before the
public push; keep the wordmark + the Left↔Center↔Right gradient as the core
visual identity.
