## Product Hunt
**Name:** NewsScope
**Tagline (≤60):** See every side of the news — in one briefing.
**Description:**
> NewsScope is a bias-aware news app. For any story, see how Left, Center, and
> Right outlets cover it side by side — and spot which side is ignoring it. Track
> your own media-diet bias over time, then round out your day with local news,
> markets, weather, and sports. Mobile-first, free to start.

**Maker's first comment:**
> Hi PH! I built NewsScope because I was tired of getting one framing of every
> story. The core feature, **Blindspot**, lines up Left/Center/Right coverage of
> the same story so you can see the spin — and the gaps. There's also a "My News
> Bias" mirror that shows how balanced *your own* reading is. It's free; Pro adds
> unlimited comparisons, alerts, and a personalized briefing. Bias ratings are
> outlet-level estimates to encourage balanced reading — not verdicts. Would love
> your feedback, especially on the comparisons. 🙏
