# NewsScope — GTM Playbook

Prioritized for a bootstrapped, trust-first launch. Effort/impact noted.

## 0. Growth engine: shareable Blindspot pages (highest ROI)
The app auto-generates public, SEO-indexed **`/story/[slug]`** pages ("How Left,
Center & Right cover X") with branded OG images. This is the core loop:
1. Pick 1–3 timely, contested stories daily.
2. Generate/curate the story page; ensure a sharp headline + OG image.
3. Seed it on X/Reddit/newsletter; build an internal **content hub** + internal
   links; target the SEO clusters below.
Why: every news cycle = fresh, evergreen-ish, highly shareable pages → compounding
organic + social traffic at ~zero marginal cost.

### SEO keyword clusters
- `media bias`, `[outlet] bias`, `left vs right news`, `balanced news app`,
  `news bias checker`, `both sides of the news`, `news comparison tool`,
  `is [outlet] biased`, `media literacy tools`, `blindspot news`.
- Programmatic: one page per major story/topic via `/story/[slug]`.

## 1. Launch beats (week 1)
- **Product Hunt** launch (assets in LAUNCH-COPY) — rally a small list first.
- **Show HN** — lead with the bias-visualization angle; engage every comment.
- **X build-in-public** thread + founder account daily posting.
Effort: M · Impact: H (spikes + backlinks + first users).

## 2. Reddit & communities (ongoing, value-first)
Subs: r/news adjacent, r/moderatepolitics, r/media_criticism,
r/InternetIsBeautiful (launch), media-literacy/teacher communities. **≥9:1
value-to-promo**, follow each sub's rules, disclose affiliation. Lead with a
genuinely useful Blindspot of a story people are arguing about.
Effort: M · Impact: M–H.

## 3. Creator / YouTube sponsorships (Ground News' #1 channel — when budget allows)
Outreach to politics/news-commentary & "centrist"/media-criticism creators.
Start with micro-creators (cheaper, engaged). Hybrid CPA + flat. Build a CRM.
Effort: M · Impact: H (defer while pre-revenue; line up the list now).

## 4. Email / newsletter
Capture from day one (footer signup + post-Blindspot CTA). Ship a **daily/weekly
"Both Sides" briefing** (the product's own briefing, emailed). Add a sponsor
line later. Lifecycle: welcome → activation nudges → trial → Pro.
Effort: M · Impact: M–H (retention + a sellable asset).

## 5. Referral
"Give a friend a month of Pro." Pro perks for invites once accounts ship.
Effort: S · Impact: M.

## 6. PWA → app stores (later)
Wrap the PWA (TWA/Capacitor) for store presence + reviews + the "install" habit.
Effort: M · Impact: M.

## Funnel & metrics
`Impressions → Visits → Signups → Activation (first Blindspot / saved location) →
Trial → Pro → Retained`. Instrument with PostHog. Watch: signup rate, activation
rate, trial→Pro, CAC by channel, organic share of new users (should rise as the
/story engine compounds).

## Cadence (steady state)
- Daily: 1–3 Blindspot stories seeded on X; community engagement.
- 3–5×/wk: X build-in-public; 1–2 Reddit value posts/wk.
- Weekly: newsletter; creator-outreach batch; metrics review.
- Monthly: a bigger "state of media bias" data piece for PR/backlinks.
